# 01 — Problem Definition

## Problem Statement
People often find public-service processes difficult because information is distributed across complicated pages, unfamiliar terminology, document requirements, and multiple steps.

## Goal
Design a simple, accessible experience that helps a person:
1. Describe what they need.
2. Find the relevant service.
3. Understand eligibility.
4. Prepare required documents.
5. Follow the process step by step.
6. Track progress.

## Core UX Question
How might we make complex public-service journeys feel as simple as completing a guided checklist?

## Success Signals
- Users understand the next step quickly.
- Users can identify required documents.
- Users can recover from mistakes.
- Users can use the experience with accessibility settings.
