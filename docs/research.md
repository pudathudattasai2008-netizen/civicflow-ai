# 02 — User Research

## Research Plan
Interview or survey students, first-time applicants, and people who have recently used public services.

## Questions
- What public service were you trying to access?
- Where did you look for information?
- What was confusing?
- Which documents were difficult to identify?
- Did you know what to do after submitting an application?
- What would make the process easier?
