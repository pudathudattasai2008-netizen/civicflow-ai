# AI Flow

## Purpose
The AI assistant helps users describe their goal in natural language and guides them toward the correct verified service information.

## Safety Principle
The AI should not invent government requirements, fees, deadlines, eligibility rules, or legal advice. Service information should come from verified sources.

## Flow
User request
→ intent extraction
→ service matching
→ verified information
→ guided next action
→ clarification when needed
